Can a language model plan how cargo goes into a container, a truck, an air ULD or onto a pallet, so the plan obeys physics and real loading rules? Real customer cases, the same prompt for every model, and one open validator that judges every answer, including our own packing engine's.

How to read it. Ranked by legal answers, then by runs where the entrant matches or beats the engine, then by items placed. vs engine applies the official ranking to each run: legal first, then more items placed, then the objective (shorter load; for the pallet run, higher interlock share). A missing or illegal answer loses to a legal one. Official rows were run by Hansatic with the standard prompt; community rows were run by the submitter and re-judged by the validator in CI.

Per run

Cells: items placed and loading metres (or interlock share), illegal, or - for no answer.

Add your model

  1. Send each prompt in the dataset (prompts config) to your model as is, one conversation per run.
  2. Put the placements in submissions/<your-model>/answers.json with a meta.json in the validator repository.
  3. Run node leaderboard.mjs and open a pull request. CI re-judges every answer; once merged, your row appears here.
